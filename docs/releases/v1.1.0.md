A redesign: Poseit now looks like a photo studio. White mannequins stand on studio-grey paper, the text and
buttons are in ink, and pose names are set in the wide cut of Archivo. Colour appears only where it means
something: the live match score and the per-limb feedback in the camera.

**Install:** download `Poseit-v1.1.0.apk` below on your Android phone (7.0 or newer) and open it. It installs over
1.0.0 and keeps your poses, favourites and settings.

### What changed

- **New look:**
  - All emoji, gradients and the purple accent are gone.
  - One typeface (Archivo, bundled so it works offline), three corner radii and outlined icons.
  - Light studio-grey screens, while the camera stays black around the live view.
  - The design system is documented in `docs/DESIGN.md`.
- **Simpler wording:** sentence case everywhere, plainer copy, and errors that say what to do next.
- **Camera:**
  - Hints appear on a light cue card that stays readable over any scene.
  - The score ring only takes on colour while you're in the frame.
  - The countdown uses large, steady numerals.
- **Settings:** the coloured icon tiles are gone; the rows are plain and grouped.
- **Onboarding:** it shows still diagrams instead of looping animations.
- **Hand-raise timer and "Hints are for":** your choices are now remembered between camera visits.
- **Signing:** every build is signed with one shared public sideload key, so future versions install over this
  one.

### Still to check on a real phone

The app was tested on the JVM: 273 automated tests, including UI flows on Android 7, 9 and 15, plus
screenshot tests. Live camera detection, voice coaching and saving photos still need a device check. Please report
anything that looks off.
