A new look, and real photos next to the figures. Poseit now has dark teal cards on a cream background, one soft lavender colour for the main button, and a new typeface. 34 of the 99 poses also show a photo of real people in the pose, beside the white figure.

**Install:** on your Android phone (7.0 or newer), download `Poseit-v1.2.0.apk` from the Assets below and open it. It installs over 1.1.0 and keeps your saved photos, your own poses, favourites and settings.

### What changed

- **New look:**
  - Dark teal cards on a cream background; cards that belong together are joined by small rounded necks.
  - One accent colour, a soft lavender, for the main button, badges and the selected option.
  - A new typeface, Hanken Grotesk (bundled, so it works offline), with big light headings.
  - White figures on dark tiles, and a new app icon.
- **Navigation:** a bar at the bottom of the home screen with the camera, a Poses and Photos switch, and a button to make your own pose.
- **Real photos:** 34 of the 99 poses show a photo of people doing the pose: on the pose's page ("See it on a person"), in the camera's pose tips, and as a small print on the library tiles and Today's pose. The figure is still what the camera matches you to; the photo shows how the pose looks on a real person.
- **Photo credits:** each photo shows its credit beside it, or, on the small prints, one tap away. Tap the credit for the author, source, licence and the changes made. All of them are in Settings > About > Photo credits.
- **Settings:** match strictness and the countdown are now at the top.
- **Large text and TalkBack:** buttons and screens adapt to large font sizes, and more controls have clear names.

Sample photos from Mixkit (Mixkit Stock Video Free License) and Wikimedia Commons (CC0 and CC BY 2.0). Full credits are in the app.

### Still to check on a real phone

The app was tested on the JVM: 374 automated tests, including UI flows on Android 7, 9 and 15, plus screenshot tests. Live camera detection, voice coaching and saving photos still need a device check. Please report anything that looks off.
