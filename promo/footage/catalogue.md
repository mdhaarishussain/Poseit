# Stock footage catalogue

Checked 2026-11-21. Free stock clips of real people for the promo film, where the app's graphics become overlays on real footage. The machine-readable version is `catalogue.json` (same data plus every detection run, hold and transition). Built by the footage scout stage; numbers come from `promo/scripts/detect.py`.

Needs: **(a)** one person, full body, posing for a camera and moving between poses, then holding one (the live-coaching act, run through the app's real detection and matcher); **(b)** different people in clear poses that match library categories, for fast pose-matched cuts; **(c)** someone photographing a friend with a phone, and reactions; **(d)** only if clearly acted and kind: someone unsure in front of a camera.

## Rules

- Never commit stock video (raw or transcoded). Previews live in promo/out/stock/ (gitignored); full-size clips go to promo/public/footage/ (*.mp4 and src/ are gitignored). Commit only this catalogue, scripts, credits and derived data.
- The people are stock models: no names, no quotes or captions that put words in their mouths, nothing that presents them as users or reviewers endorsing Poseit, nothing that mocks them or shows them in a bad light. Generic on-screen copy in the app's voice is fine.
- Mixkit: only items whose own page says Mixkit Stock Video Free License. Items under the Mixkit Stock Video Restricted License are personal-use only and are listed under excluded.
- Coverr: only stock-library items with isPremium false and isAiGenerated false (AI-generated people are not real humans; premium items, which include Coverr's vertical footage, need a paid plan).

## Need (a): the best three

**1. `mk-34404`**, Mixkit, [Woman posing for the camera in the middle of a desert](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/woman-posing-for-the-camera-in-the-middle-of-a-34404/), 1080x1920 vertical, 10.7 s. The app's lite model finds her in every frame with hips, knees and ankles at 0.98 mean visibility. She moves through three clearly different front-facing poses with clean transitions: arms down (hold 0.96-3.58 s at 720p), a fast move (3.58-5.42 s, peak 121 deg/s), both hands behind the head (hold 5.42-7.88 s), then a slower change to one hand high and one behind the head (hold 9.21-10.54 s). Free Mixkit licence. Limits: 10.7 s; the loose dress hides the knees a little; desert at dusk, not a studio. Use: The live-coaching act: the guide over her, the score climbing through the arm move at 3.6-5.4 s, the lock and countdown over the 5.4-7.9 s hold.

**2. `mk-39676`**, Mixkit, [Girl in a photographic set modeling on a pink background](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/girl-in-a-photographic-set-modeling-on-a-pink-background-39676/), 1080x1920 vertical, 16.0 s. **3. `mk-52257`**, Mixkit, [A young beautiful model poses with poise and elegance wearing a white attire and red boots over a white background](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/a-young-beautiful-model-poses-with-poise-and-elegance-wearing-52257/), 1920x1080 landscape, 17.0 s. ## How this was made, and how to rerun it

- Search: Mixkit tag pages (posing, model, fashion, photographer, selfie, full-body) and search pages (https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/discover/<query>/: pose, posing, model-posing, photoshoot, taking-photo, woman-posing, man-posing, couple-posing, friends-taking-photo, fashion-model, full-body, phone-photo, shy, awkward, nervous, confused, group-photo and others): 1,194 unique items read from the grids. Coverr search pages (https://coverr.co/s?q=<query>: posing, model, photoshoot, taking photos, photographs friend, taking photo phone, selfie, posing for photo, fashion model, full body, photo shoot studio, dancing studio): 269 items, of which 54 premium and 30 AI-generated were dropped.
- Previews: 360p previews of 56 candidates (43 Mixkit, 13 Coverr) in promo/out/stock/<id>.mp4; a 12-frame contact sheet per clip in promo/out/stock/sheets/<id>.jpg (timestamps burned in), each looked at.
- Detection: promo/scripts/detect.py with the app's live-analyzer model: app/src/main/assets/models/pose_landmarker_lite.task (Settings > accurate model is off by default; PoseDetector.kt MODEL_LITE), min detection/presence/tracking confidence 0.5, frames fed un-mirrored, VIDEO running mode (the app uses LIVE_STREAM on camera frames; VIDEO keeps the same tracking for a file). num_poses 4 to count people (the app uses the guide's person count). Run on every 360p preview; the strongest need-(a) clips and the landscape clips that need a 9:16 crop were also run at 720p, and the top three with the full model too.
- Stats: detection_rate: frames with a person. full_body_rate: frames where the followed person's hips, knees and ankles average visibility >= 0.6, both ankles >= 0.5 and the nose >= 0.5. lower_body_visibility_mean: mean visibility of hips, knees, ankles. people_max/mode: people found. pose_speed: mean angular speed of 10 segments (8 limb bones, shoulder line, hip line) over a 0.25 s lag, deg/s; jitter on a still body is about 3-8, a clear move 40+. holds: full body and pose speed < 15 deg/s for >= 0.5 s. transitions: the stretches between holds. Followed person: the largest, or (subject fullest) the most fully visible, for over-the-shoulder shots.
- Setup: `python3 -m venv promo/.venv && promo/.venv/bin/pip install mediapipe opencv-python-headless numpy` (MediaPipe 1.2.3 here; on a bare Linux image it also needs the system `libegl1` and `libgles2`).
- Rerun one clip: `promo/.venv/bin/python promo/scripts/detect.py promo/out/stock/<id>.mp4 -o promo/out/stock/landmarks/<id>.json --summary promo/out/stock/summary/<id>.json --num-poses 4 [--subject fullest] [--crop x,0,w,1]`. Re-summarize a saved run with other thresholds: `promo/.venv/bin/python promo/scripts/detect.py --from-json <landmarks.json> --hold-speed 15 --subject fullest`.
- The per-frame landmark files and summaries are in `promo/out/stock/landmarks/` and `promo/out/stock/summary/` (gitignored, rebuilt by the command above). For the film, rerun `detect.py` on the full-size clip in `promo/public/footage/` with the crop the edit uses, and feed those landmarks to the app's matcher.
